Who am I?

My name is Mauricio Renato Sarda Filho. I'm a web developer at the start of my career, eager to learn and to build. My path into technology began with hardware maintenance and video editing, and it now expands into the world of web development.

I currently focus on front-end — building semantic, responsive and accessible interfaces. I believe good code and good design go hand in hand.
